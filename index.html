My own project · Public information

mySekilas

A free website where anyone in Malaysia can find out who their elected representatives are, which party runs their state, and what is in the news there. In Malay and English.

The problem

To find out who represents you and what is happening in your state, you would have to dig through government websites, election results and many news sites.

What I built

  • A map of Malaysia you can search by area, postcode or name
  • A "Who is my YB?" button that finds your representatives from your location, without your location ever leaving your phone or computer
  • How many seats each party holds in Parliament, and who runs each state, checked against official government sites
  • News collected automatically from many Malaysian news sites, with the same story from different sites shown together and sorted by state and topic
  • Short AI-written summaries of big stories, and a daily round-up
  • A guide to who to contact about a problem, and a short political history

Built with Laravel, Vite, Google Gemini API, Render

A private personal organiser: track spending against a monthly budget, keep notes and write a diary, all in one app. Published on Google Play, with a web version too, in English and Malay.

AI report writer for PR teams

NUJUM

A tool for public relations and communications teams. Give it a topic or a social media account, answer a few questions, and it writes a report on what people are saying and what may happen next, listing every source it used.

Client
Intensiti Elemen

The problem

Going through news, posts and documents by hand took so long that warnings and advice often came too late.

What I built

  • Six kinds of report, from forecasts and social media account checks to fact-checking a claim
  • Teams can upload their own documents and ask questions about them, and each answer shows where the facts came from
  • A morning briefing and one screen that tracks every issue the team is watching
  • Reports to download or share by link, with a free trial and paid monthly plans for teams

The result

Work that used to be slow and done by hand now takes minutes, and every report lists the news, posts and files it was based on.

Built with Laravel, MySQL, Google Gemini API, OpenAI API, ToyyibPay

Online ticket sales

BINA e-ticketing

The website where people sign up and buy tickets for BINA, a construction industry conference for Southeast Asia, held at MITEC in Kuala Lumpur.

Client
CIDB IBS

The problem

Sign-ups and payments were handled by hand, which was slow and hard to keep track of with so many attendees.

What I built

  • A page for each event in the conference
  • Online sign-up and payment by online banking or card
  • Tickets sent to buyers digitally, with a record of what they bought
  • A back office where organisers see every order and ticket

The result

Signing up, paying and getting a ticket all happen online now, and organisers manage everything from one place. They came back to use it again for the 2026 conference.

Built with Laravel, MySQL, ToyyibPay, Stripe

About
