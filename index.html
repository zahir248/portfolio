Personal project · Civic information · Web

mySekilas

An interactive map of Malaysia that shows who governs each state, who your MP and state assemblyman are, and what is in the news there, in Malay and English.

Role
Solo: design, build, data and deploy

Problem

Who represents you, and what is happening in your state, is spread across Parliament pages, election results and many news portals.

What I built

  • A map of states, parliamentary seats and state seats, with search by area, postcode or name
  • A "Who is my YB?" button that matches your location inside the browser and never sends it to the server
  • Dewan Rakyat seat counts and each state's government, checked against official portals
  • Live headlines from many Malaysian portals, grouped when several cover the same story and tagged by state and topic
  • Story summaries and a daily round-up written with Google Gemini
  • A guide to who to contact for a problem, and a political history section

Outcome

One page answers who represents me and what is happening near me, and every figure links to where it came from.

Laravel · Vite · Google Gemini API · Render

AI analysis platform · Web

NUJUM

Pick one of six kinds of analysis, answer a few questions, and get a report that names its sources, in Malay or English.

Role
Full-stack, solo
Client
Intensiti Elemen
Timeline
Aug 2025 to Jul 2026

Problem

Checking data by hand took too long, so risks and useful advice often came too late.

What I built

  • Six analysis types: prediction, trend topic, social profile, profile comparison, communication pack and fact check
  • A document library with an Ask feature that answers from the user's own files and shows where each fact came from
  • A war room, daily brief and stakeholder tracking for communications teams
  • Reports as PDF or shareable links, a free tier, and monthly plans with usage allowances and team accounts

Outcome

Analysis that used to be done by hand now runs on demand, and every report lists the news, posts and files it was written from.

Laravel · MySQL · Google Gemini API · OpenAI API · ToyyibPay
